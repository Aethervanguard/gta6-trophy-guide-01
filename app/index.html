      Roadmap to the platinum

      One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no mission gold medals, no mission replay — but free-roam stays open after the story, so almost everything can be cleaned up afterwards. The one thing to do on time is in Stage 1.

          Missable trophies

          Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else stays available in free-roam after the story.

            Why the rest are safe

            • Story trophies pop automatically as you play; there is no mission replay, but no story trophy can be skipped.
            • Optional missions sometimes expire (hourglass icon) and some quest givers vanish for a while — Kenton, for example, goes quiet after Chapter 1 and returns in Chapters 2–3. All trophy-related characters are available again later, so nothing locks you out.
            • Some fences disappear for good and a few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that become inaccessible — neither affects any trophy or the 100% checklist.
            • Keep manual saves. The game has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave slot; keep a save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotate the rest for free-roam cleanup.
            • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off until the platinum pops.
